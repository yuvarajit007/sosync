SLIDE 1

Problem Statement

Current Emergency Challenges:

  • Victims of severe accidents are often in shock, panicked, or unconscious, unable to speak or give coordinates over voice calls.
  • Emergency dispatchers waste 3-5 critical minutes ("Golden Hour") asking "Where are you? What kind of fire? How many injured?".
  • Wrong responder types are frequently dispatched (e.g. fire truck sent when heavy rescue extrication gear was needed).
SLIDE 2

Proposed Solution

SOSync AI Architecture:

  • Zero-Friction Trigger: 1-touch SOS initiates autonomous capture without blocking the user.
  • Multi-Frame Evidence: 5 rapid burst photos over ~5 seconds document vehicle angles, injuries, and fire progression.
  • AI Scene Intelligence: Multi-modal vision identifies crash severity, victim count, and hazardous leaks in under 500ms.
  • Fail-Safe Verification: Allows 1-tap confirmation or 10-second automatic dispatch if victim passes out.
SLIDE 3

Smart Routing & Multi-Agency Dispatch

Unified Response Orchestration:

  • Routes structured dossiers simultaneously to 108 (Ambulance), 100 (Police), and 101 (Fire & Rescue).
  • Provides responders with turn-by-turn navigation, scene photographs, and pre-arrival triage equipment recommendations.
  • Saves an estimated 4 to 6 minutes in critical response time during the trauma golden hour.
SLIDE 4

Judge Q&A Defense Key Points

Anticipated Questions & Winning Answers:

  • Q: What if there is no internet in the accident zone?
    A: SOSync includes an SMS / USSD / LoRa fall-back compression packet that encodes GPS coordinates and AI category into a 140-character encrypted SMS string.
  • Q: What if the AI misidentifies the incident?
    A: The 5 raw photos and GPS are always attached unedited; responders always inspect raw visual ground truth. Human verification has 1-tap override.
  • Q: What if the victim is unconscious?
    A: The 10-second fail-safe timer automatically dispatches the report if no touch is detected, preventing delays.
